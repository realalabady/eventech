# CLAUDE_SYSTEM_THINKING.md

# EvenTech
## System Thinking Principles

---

# Goal

Build systems.

Not pages.

Not features.

Not isolated modules.

---

# Every Feature Must Connect To

Master Data

↓

Operations

↓

Finance

↓

CRM

↓

Analytics

↓

Reports

↓

Archive

---

# Every Entity

Should answer

Who owns it?

Where is it used?

Who can edit it?

Who can approve it?

Who reports on it?

What depends on it?

---

# Every Module

Must provide

Search

Filters

Permissions

Activity Logs

Reports

Notifications

Attachments

Comments

History

---

# Before Creating New Data

Ask

Can this reference existing data?

Can this be reused?

Can this become a template?

Can this be automated?

---

# Product Principle

One change

↓

Updates everywhere.

One source

↓

Many consumers.

---

# Success

The platform behaves as one connected ecosystem instead of many disconnected pages.
